💡 TIPS FOR FARMERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ LOOK AT COLORS FIRST:
   - See red? = Danger! Take action now
   - See yellow? = Important, pay attention
   - See green? = Good, healthy, safe

✅ LOOK AT ICONS:
   - Every button has a picture (emoji)
   - Picture tells you what it does
   - Don't need to read if you recognize the icon

✅ ONE TAP IS ENOUGH:
   - Buttons are BIG - easy to tap
   - No tiny buttons anywhere
   - If you can't tap it easily, it's not done right!

✅ TOP TO BOTTOM:
   - Read from top to bottom (natural)
   - No left-right reading needed
   - Everything flows down the screen

✅ TAKE YOUR TIME:
   - App shows one thing at a time
   - Not too much information
   - Focus on what matters right now

✨ WHAT MAKES THIS FARMER-FRIENDLY?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. 🎨 COLORS = MEANING
   Green/Yellow/Red is universal. Traffic light system.
   Everyone knows red = stop/danger, green = go/good.

2. 📱 BIG EVERYTHING
   Text, buttons, icons - all LARGE. Easy to see, easy 
   to tap. No squinting, no precision aiming needed.

   Not overwhelming. Show one important thing. Farmer 
   focuses on that. Then moves to next.

4. 🔤 SIMPLE WORDS
   "Check Leaf" not "Diagnostic Analysis"
   "Sick" not "Pathogen Detected"
   "My Profit" not "Yield Optimization Calculator"

5. 🎭 ICONS EVERYWHERE
   Camera for photo
   Money bag for profit
   Warning sign for danger
   Map pin for location
   → Universal symbols everyone knows

6. 📐 VERTICAL LAYOUT
   Everything stacks top-to-bottom. Natural reading flow.
   No confusing side-by-side panels. One column, simple.

7. 🌞 OUTDOOR FRIENDLY
   Cream background (not white) - easier on eyes in bright sun
   High contrast text - can read even in harsh sunlight
   No tiny text that disappears outdoors
