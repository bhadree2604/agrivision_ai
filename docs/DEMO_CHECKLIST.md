## Demo Flow (2-3 minutes)

### 🎬 Opening (15 seconds)
**Say:** "AgriVision AI is a smart agriculture platform for Tamil Nadu farmers, helping them diagnose plant diseases, track regional outbreaks, find local resources, and optimize crop yields."

**Show:** Main screen with header and logo visible

---

### 🔬 Tab 1: AI Diagnostic Hub (45 seconds)

**Actions:**
1. ✅ Click "Tomato Spot" sample image (middle thumbnail)
2. ✅ Watch 3-second AI scanning animation
3. ✅ Point out results: Disease name, confidence 92.3%, severity, treatment steps
4. ✅ Scroll to show recovery timeline

**Say:** "Farmers can upload leaf photos or use our quick samples. The AI analyzes in 3 seconds and provides actionable treatment protocols with recovery timelines."

**Key Feature:** Instant disease diagnosis without expert knowledge

---

### 📊 Tab 2: Regional Analytics (30 seconds)

**Actions:**
1. ✅ Click "Regional Analytics" tab
2. ✅ Wait 1 second for skeleton → charts animation
3. ✅ Point to line chart: "Disease spread over 4 weeks"
4. ✅ Point to pie chart: "Affected crop distribution"
5. ✅ Highlight red risk alert at bottom

**Say:** "Real-time outbreak tracking helps farmers stay ahead of disease spread in Namakkal and Rasipuram districts. High-risk alerts recommend preventive action."

**Key Feature:** Predictive risk assessment

---

### 🗺️ Tab 3: Local Resources (30 seconds)

**Actions:**
1. ✅ Click "Local Resources" tab
2. ✅ Click a map pin (e.g., Namakkal Supply Center)
3. ✅ Watch smooth zoom + sidebar highlight animation
4. ✅ Point out: Phone number, stock status, distance

**Say:** "Interactive map shows nearby supply centers, cold storage, and testing labs. Click any pin to see contact details and stock availability—crucial for time-sensitive farming needs."

**Key Feature:** Hyperlocal resource discovery

---

### 💰 Tab 4: Yield Optimizer (30 seconds)

**Actions:**
1. ✅ Click "Yield Optimizer" tab
2. ✅ Enter: Acreage = 5, Crop = Tomato, Season = Kharif
3. ✅ Click "Calculate Profit"
4. ✅ Watch ROI progress bar animate
5. ✅ Show net profit and recommendation

**Say:** "Farmers enter their land size and crop choice to get instant profit projections, cost breakdowns, and smart recommendations—helping them make data-driven farming decisions."

**Key Feature:** ROI calculation & optimization

## 🚨 Troubleshooting During Demo

### If sample image doesn't load:
- Use webcam or upload a random image
- Results will still show (it's mock data)

### If map doesn't load:
- Mention: "Map requires internet—normally shows 5 resource centers"
- Skip to yield optimizer

### If charts don't appear:
- Refresh page (should take 5 seconds)
- Have backup tab ready

### If translation breaks:
- Refresh and skip language demo
- Focus on core features
